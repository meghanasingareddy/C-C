Behind the Counter

Our Story & Founder Vision

"Growing up in India, two things were permanent fixtures in our homes: a perpetually simmering pot of masala chai and shelves overflowing with paperbacks."

We built Chai & Chapter to recreate the warmth of home reading. In a fast-paced digital world, we wanted to create a sanctuary where people can slow down, turn paper pages, sip unhurried tea, and form meaningful community connections.

"A book store gives you knowledge; a café gives you warmth. Chai & Chapter brings both together under one roof."

— Ananya & Vikram (Founders)

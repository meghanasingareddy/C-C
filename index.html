ABOUT US

More Than Just A Café

Chai for the heart.
Books for the soul.

Chai & Chapter was born from a simple yearning: a quiet haven in Hyderabad where time slows down, phones can rest, and humans can connect with good literature and warm chai.

Whether you're looking for an uninterrupted 3-hour reading session, a cozy spot to write your novel, or a heartfelt conversation over pizza and filter coffee, there's always a warm seat waiting for you.

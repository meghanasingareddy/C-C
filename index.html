YOUR SANCTUARY EXPERIENCE

How Chai & Chapter Works

Four simple steps to your most peaceful afternoon in Hyderabad.

01

Reserve Your Nook

Choose your preferred reading vibe online — completely free of charge. Your nook is held for 15 minutes past your arrival.

02

Pick Any Free Book

Select any title from our curated 18+ books or browse 2,000+ shelves in person. Free to read during your stay.

03

Sip & Savor

Order freshly brewed kulhad chai, signature pour-overs, and artisan bites delivered right to your nook.

04

Read, Unplug & Repeat

No rush, no hourly timers. Stay as long as your chapter takes, connect to fast Wi-Fi, and join our community.

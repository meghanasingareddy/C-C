# Chai & Chapter - Book Cafe Website

Chai & Chapter is a business website for a book cafe that combines authentic Indian chai culture with dedicated reading nooks, artisanal beverages, and a community space for book lovers and writers.

This project is built using HTML5 and CSS3.

## Key Features

### 1. Reading Nook Reservation System
Patrons can reserve specific reading spaces tailored to their mood and work requirements:
* **The Baithak:** Low Indian-style floor cushion seating with low tea tables (High Quiet Zone).
* **The Sunlit Bay Window:** Natural sunlight armchair nook overlooking garden greenery (Medium Quiet Zone).
* **The Library Alcove:** Private desk tucked between antique bookshelves with desk light and high-speed WiFi (Silent Zone).
* **The Courtyard Swing:** Wooden swing under indoor greenery and ambient lighting (Ambient Quiet Zone).
* Includes a reservation request form with date, time slot, nook choice, genre preference, and special requests.

### 2. Cafe Menu
* **Signature Chai and Teas:** Desi Kulhad Masala Chai, Adrak Elaichi Special, Royal Kashmiri Kahwa, Old Bombay Irani Chai.
* **Artisanal Coffees:** South Indian Filter Kaapi, Hazelnut Cinnamon Latte, Overnight Dark Roast Cold Brew, Cardamom Rose Espresso Tonic.
* **Bookworm Bakes and Savories:** Warm Bun Maska with House Jam, Spiced Paneer and Corn Toastie, Baked Cocktail Samosa Sliders, Date, Fig and Walnut Loaf Cake.

### 3. Community Events
Displays upcoming cafe gatherings with instant RSVP pass triggers:
* **Shaam-e-Shayari:** Candlelight poetry night with ghazals and open mic slots.
* **Author Spotlight:** Q&A session and book signing for independent Indian fiction.
* **The Sunday Book Swap:** Bring one, take one book exchange morning.

### 4. Founder Story
Introduces the founders, Ananya and Vikram, sharing their vision of recreating the warmth of home reading in a slow-living sanctuary.

### 5. Reader Reviews
Displays real reader testimonials with star ratings and reader category badges.

### 6. Newsletter Subscription (The Chapter Club)
Email subscription form for monthly curated reading lists, priority nook booking passes, and birthday rewards.

## Technologies Used

* HTML5 (Semantic Structure)
* CSS3 (Responsive Layout, Flexbox, CSS Grid, Custom Properties, Warm Parchment Theme)
